# Grade 6 Mathematics — Bridging Curriculum
**Ontario entry level → ICSE standard · 10-month cohort · 3 live classes/week + assignment after each**

## Design principle
Grade 6 is where the gap is widest — Ontario delays **both** algebra and
statistics to Grade 8, while ICSE treats both as full Grade 6 chapters. A student
entering from Ontario Grade 6 typically has solid arithmetic and no abstraction.
Term 1 therefore rebuilds number fluency *with negative numbers* (the missing
prerequisite for algebra), and only then opens the algebra chapter.

**Non-negotiable rule:** no new chapter begins until its prerequisite diagnostic is passed.

---

## Term 1 — Number Systems & Prerequisites (Weeks 1–10)

### Weeks 1–2 · Diagnostic + Number Review
- **Prerequisite check:** fractions (all four operations), factors/multiples, decimals
- Place value, estimation, order of operations (**BODMAS**)
- *Any student failing the fractions diagnostic gets a parallel remediation set alongside Term 1 — they are not left behind, and the cohort is not slowed.*

### Weeks 3–5 · Integers ⭐ *(critical algebra prerequisite)*
- Negative numbers on the number line; ordering and comparing
- Addition and subtraction of integers (number-line and rule-based)
- Multiplication and division of integers; sign rules
- Integer word problems (temperature, elevation, profit/loss)
- *Why it's here:* you cannot do algebra without confident negatives. Ontario introduces integer operations in Grade 7.

### Weeks 6–8 · Factors, Multiples & Number Theory
- Prime factorisation, HCF and LCM (both methods)
- Divisibility rules; tests for 7, 11
- Applications: LCM for fractions, HCF for simplification
- Squares, square roots, cubes (introduction)

### Weeks 9–10 · Fractions & Decimals Mastery
- All four operations with complex/mixed fractions
- Decimal operations including division by decimals
- Fraction ↔ decimal ↔ percentage triangle
- **Term 1 assessment + parent progress report**

---

## Term 2 — Algebra & Proportional Reasoning (Weeks 11–22)

### Weeks 11–15 · Algebra ⭐⭐ *(the signature chapter)*
**Full ICSE Grade 6 algebra chapter. Two years ahead of Ontario.**
- Constants, variables, algebraic expressions; terms and coefficients
- Like and unlike terms; addition/subtraction of expressions
- Multiplication of a monomial by a monomial/binomial
- Formation of expressions from word statements
- **Linear equations in one variable**: solving by balancing
- Equations with brackets; variables on both sides
- Word problems → equation → solution → verification
- *Gap closed:* **+2 years.**

### Weeks 16–18 · Ratio, Proportion & Percentage
- Ratio in simplest form; dividing in a ratio; continued ratio
- Direct and inverse proportion; unitary method
- Percentage: increase/decrease, profit and loss (introduction)
- Simple interest (introduction)

### Weeks 19–22 · Data Handling & Statistics ⭐
**Full statistics chapter — Ontario delays this to Grade 8.**
- Raw data, frequency distribution tables, tally marks
- Bar graphs, double bar graphs, pie chart reading
- **Mean, median, mode** — computation and interpretation
- Choosing the right average for a data set; effect of outliers
- *Gap closed:* **+2 years.** This is also the direct foundation for the statistics underlying machine learning.
- **Term 2 assessment + parent progress report**

---

## Term 3 — Geometry & Consolidation (Weeks 23–34)

### Weeks 23–26 · Geometry
- Angle pairs: complementary, supplementary, adjacent, vertically opposite
- Parallel lines with a transversal — corresponding, alternate, co-interior angles
- Triangle properties: angle sum, exterior angle theorem
- Triangle classification and inequality
- Quadrilateral properties and angle sum

### Weeks 27–29 · Mensuration
- Perimeter and area: rectangle, square, triangle, parallelogram
- Area of compound figures
- Circumference and area of a circle (introduction to π)
- Surface area and volume of cube and cuboid

### Weeks 30–31 · Geometric Constructions
- Compass constructions: perpendicular bisector, angle bisector
- Constructing angles (60°, 90°, 30°, 45°) without a protractor
- Constructing triangles from given conditions

### Weeks 32–34 · Revision & Grade 7 Readiness
- Full-syllabus revision with mixed problem sets
- **Grade 7 prerequisite diagnostic** (must pass to advance)
- Final assessment + end-of-year report

## Assignment structure (3 per week)
| After class | Assignment type | Approx. time |
|---|---|---|
| Class 1 | Concept practice — routine drilling of the new skill | 20 min |
| Class 2 | Mixed practice — new topic + spaced revision | 25–30 min |
| Class 3 | Word problems / applications — full written solutions | 30 min |

**Algebra weeks carry extra weight:** during Weeks 11–15 the third assignment is
always word-problem-to-equation translation, because that is the specific skill
students lose without repeated practice.

## Exit standard
A student completing Grade 6 here can: solve linear equations including brackets
and variables on both sides, translate word problems into equations reliably,
compute and interpret mean/median/mode, work confidently with negative numbers,
apply angle rules on parallel lines, and construct standard angles with compass.
