# Grade 5 Mathematics — Bridging Curriculum
**Ontario entry level → ICSE standard · 10-month cohort · 3 live classes/week + assignment after each**

## Design principle
A Grade 5 student arriving from an Ontario school has *not* seen variables, formal
ratio notation, or systematic factorisation. Dropping them straight into ICSE
Grade 5 material fails. So the first six weeks deliberately re-teach Ontario-level
content **using ICSE notation and rigour** — same topics they already know, higher
standard of writing and reasoning. The gap is closed by pacing, not by skipping.

**Non-negotiable rule:** no new chapter begins until its prerequisite diagnostic is passed.

---

## Term 1 — Foundation & Number Sense (Weeks 1–10)

### Weeks 1–2 · Diagnostic + Place Value to Millions
- **Prerequisite check:** 4-digit addition/subtraction, times tables to 10
- Place value to 10,000,000; Indian *and* international numbering systems
- Rounding, estimation, comparing large numbers
- *Bridge note:* Ontario covers this to 100,000. We extend the range and introduce the Indian lakh/crore system as a second notation.

### Weeks 3–5 · Operations & Factorisation
- Long multiplication (3-digit × 2-digit), long division with remainders
- **Factors, multiples, prime numbers**
- **HCF and LCM** — listing method, then prime factorisation method
- Divisibility rules (2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11)
- *Gap closed:* Ontario introduces HCF/LCM in Grade 6. This is the first real acceleration.

### Weeks 6–8 · Fractions
- Equivalent fractions, simplification to lowest terms
- Addition/subtraction with **unlike denominators** (via LCM — connects to Weeks 3–5)
- Multiplication of fractions; fraction of a quantity
- Improper fractions ↔ mixed numbers

### Weeks 9–10 · Decimals
- Place value to thousandths; decimal ↔ fraction conversion
- All four operations with decimals
- **Term 1 assessment + parent progress report**

---

## Term 2 — Introducing Abstraction (Weeks 11–22)

### Weeks 11–13 · Ratio & Proportion *(first ICSE-ahead topic)*
- Ratio notation, equivalent ratios, simplifying
- Dividing a quantity in a given ratio
- Unitary method (direct proportion word problems)
- *Gap closed:* Ontario formalises ratio in Grade 6–7. **+1.5 years.**

### Weeks 14–17 · Introduction to Algebra ⭐
**The signature chapter of Grade 5 here.**
- Variables as unknowns; why letters replace boxes
- Writing algebraic expressions from word statements
- Evaluating expressions by substitution
- Simple one-step equations (x + 7 = 12, 3x = 21)
- Forming equations from word problems
- *Gap closed:* Ontario does not introduce variables until Grade 7. **+2 years.**

### Weeks 18–20 · Geometry & Measurement
- Lines, rays, angle types; measuring/constructing with protractor
- Triangle and quadrilateral classification; **angle sum of a triangle**
- Perimeter and area: rectangle, square, compound shapes
- Introduction to the coordinate grid (first quadrant)

### Weeks 21–22 · Data Handling
- Pictographs, bar graphs, double bar graphs
- **Average (arithmetic mean)** — introduced here, Ontario in Grade 6
- **Term 2 assessment + parent progress report**

---

## Term 3 — Consolidation & Reasoning (Weeks 23–34)

### Weeks 23–25 · Percentage (Introduction)
- Percentage as fraction/decimal; conversions
- Finding a percentage of a quantity
- *Gap closed:* Ontario introduces percentage in Grade 6–7.

### Weeks 26–28 · Symmetry, Nets & Solids
- Lines of symmetry, reflection
- 3-D solids: faces, edges, vertices; nets
- Volume of a cube and cuboid

### Weeks 29–31 · Multi-Step Problem Solving ⭐
- Problems combining fractions + ratio + percentage
- Working backwards; logical elimination
- **Writing out full solution reasoning** — this is the ICSE examination habit and is graded on presentation, not just the answer

### Weeks 32–34 · Revision & Grade 6 Readiness
- Full-syllabus revision
- **Grade 6 prerequisite diagnostic** (must pass to advance)
- Final assessment + end-of-year report

---

## Assignment structure (3 per week)
| After class | Assignment type | Approx. time |
|---|---|---|
| Class 1 | Concept practice — 8–10 routine questions | 20 min |
| Class 2 | Mixed practice — new topic + spaced revision of an older one | 25 min |
| Class 3 | Word problems / reasoning — full written solutions | 30 min |

**Spaced revision is deliberate:** every third assignment revisits a topic from
3–4 weeks earlier. This is what makes the foundation stick rather than fade.

## Exit standard
A student completing Grade 5 here can: form and solve one-step equations from
word problems, work fluently with fractions using LCM, find HCF/LCM by prime
factorisation, handle ratio and unitary-method problems, and write out a
multi-step solution with visible reasoning.
