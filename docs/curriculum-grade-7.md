# Grade 7 Mathematics — Bridging Curriculum
**Ontario entry level → ICSE standard · 10-month cohort · 3 live classes/week + assignment after each**

## Design principle
Grade 7 is the bridge year. The goal is not just "ahead" — it is that the student
walks into Grade 9 already fluent in the two things that break most students
there: **formal algebraic manipulation** and **deductive proof**. Ontario meets
both in Grade 9. We meet them here, with two extra years to consolidate.

**Non-negotiable rule:** no new chapter begins until its prerequisite diagnostic is passed.

---

## Term 1 — Algebraic Fluency (Weeks 1–11)

### Weeks 1–2 · Diagnostic + Integers & Rationals
- **Prerequisite check:** integer operations, one-step equations, fraction fluency
- Rational numbers: representation, comparison, operations
- Properties: commutative, associative, distributive
- *Students entering without confident negatives get parallel remediation — this cannot be skipped, everything downstream depends on it.*

### Weeks 3–5 · Exponents & Powers ⭐
- Exponential notation; base and index
- **Laws of exponents** (product, quotient, power of a power, zero and negative indices)
- Expressing large/small numbers in **standard form (scientific notation)**
- *Gap closed:* Ontario formalises exponent laws in Grade 9. **+2 years.**

### Weeks 6–8 · Algebraic Expressions
- Terms, coefficients, degree; like/unlike terms
- Addition, subtraction, multiplication of polynomials
- Multiplication of binomials (FOIL / distributive expansion)
- Division of a polynomial by a monomial

### Weeks 9–11 · Algebraic Identities ⭐⭐
**The signature chapter. Ontario meets this in Grade 9–10.**
- (a + b)², (a − b)², (a + b)(a − b)
- Applying identities to expand efficiently
- **Factorisation**: common factor, grouping, using identities, trinomials
- Numerical shortcuts via identities (e.g. 103² mentally)
- *Gap closed:* **+2 to +3 years.**
- **Term 1 assessment + parent progress report**

---

## Term 2 — Equations, Proof & Proportion (Weeks 12–23)

### Weeks 12–14 · Linear Equations & Inequalities
- Equations with brackets, fractions, variables on both sides
- Word problems: ages, numbers, money, distance–speed–time
- **Linear inequalities**: solving and representing on a number line
- Verification as a required step (ICSE examination habit)

### Weeks 15–18 · Geometry: Congruence & Proof ⭐⭐
**Formal deductive proof — Ontario delays this to Grade 9.**
- Congruence of triangles: **SSS, SAS, ASA, RHS** criteria
- Writing formal two-column proofs with justifications
- Properties of isosceles and equilateral triangles
- Pythagoras' theorem: statement, application, converse
- *Gap closed:* **+2 years.** This chapter builds the deductive-reasoning habit — arguably the single most transferable skill in the whole programme.

### Weeks 19–21 · Percentage & Financial Math
- Profit, loss, discount, successive discount
- **Simple interest**; introduction to compound interest
- Percentage increase/decrease; reverse percentage
- Real-world financial word problems

### Weeks 22–23 · Ratio, Proportion & Variation
- Direct and inverse variation
- Time and work; time, speed and distance
- Compound proportion
- **Term 2 assessment + parent progress report**

---

## Term 3 — Advanced Topics & High-School Readiness (Weeks 24–34)

### Weeks 24–26 · Mensuration
- Area: triangle (including Heron's formula), parallelogram, rhombus, trapezium
- Circle: circumference, area, sector area
- Surface area and volume: cube, cuboid, cylinder

### Weeks 27–29 · Data Handling & Probability
- Mean, median, mode for grouped and ungrouped data
- Range; interpreting data spread
- Bar graphs, histograms, pie charts (construction, not just reading)
- **Introduction to probability**: sample space, simple events
- *Note:* probability + statistics here is the direct mathematical basis of machine learning — worth naming explicitly to students.

### Weeks 30–31 · Coordinate Geometry
- Cartesian plane, all four quadrants
- Plotting points; distance between points on axes
- Graphing simple linear relationships — **the bridge to Grade 9 functions**

### Weeks 32–34 · Revision & Grade 8/9 Readiness
- Full-syllabus revision, mixed and cumulative papers
- **Grade 8 readiness diagnostic**
- Final assessment + end-of-year report

## Assignment structure (3 per week)
| After class | Assignment type | Approx. time |
|---|---|---|
| Class 1 | Skill drilling — algebraic manipulation, identities, factorisation | 25 min |
| Class 2 | Mixed practice + spaced revision of earlier chapters | 30 min |
| Class 3 | Proof writing / multi-step applications — full solutions | 30–35 min |

**Proof weeks (15–18) are the highest-effort stretch of the year.** Assignments
require full written justification for every step; partial marks are given for
reasoning even when the final answer is wrong. This is deliberate — it trains the
habit that determines success in Grade 9 and beyond.

## Exit standard
A student completing Grade 7 here can: expand and factorise using standard
identities, apply all exponent laws including negative indices, write a valid
two-column congruence proof, solve linear equations and inequalities from word
problems, apply Pythagoras confidently, and plot and interpret linear
relationships on the Cartesian plane — i.e. they arrive at Grade 9 having already
met Grade 9's hardest content once.
